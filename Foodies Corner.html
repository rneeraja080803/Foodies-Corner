Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani

View Recipe

Ingredients: Basmati rice, chicken, yogurt, fried onions, ginger-garlic paste, tomatoes, biryani masala, saffron milk, mint & coriander, ghee.

Steps: Marinate chicken in yogurt and spices for 30 minutes. Cook with onions and tomatoes into a rich gravy. Parboil rice separately with whole spices. Layer rice over the gravy, top with fried onions, mint, coriander, and saffron milk. Cover and cook on low heat (dum) for 20–25 minutes. Fluff gently and serve hot.

Irresistible and rich flavors.
